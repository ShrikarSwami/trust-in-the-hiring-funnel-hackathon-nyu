## The Challenge: Trust in the Hiring Funnel

Hiring has a trust crisis on both sides of the funnel — recruiters can't tell which applicants
are real, and candidates can't tell which employers reaching out are real. Generative AI has
broken both halves of that assumption at once. The job for the day: rebuild it. Teams can
attack either side, or the seam between them.

### Side A — Trust coming in: the applicant flood

A role that used to draw 30 applicants now draws 500, and a growing share aren't real, aren't
qualified, or are gaming the system. Candidates embed hidden prompt-injection text to
manipulate AI resume-screening tools. Applicants increasingly look strikingly alike — same
phrasing, same structure, same accomplishments — a tell that everyone is leaning on the same AI
tools. Recruiters are drowning; no clear winning tool yet.

**Challenge:** reliably separate real, qualified humans from AI-inflated noise — without
punishing legitimate candidates who simply used AI to polish their resume.

Questions worth chasing:
- What signals (behavioral, identity, provenance) actually distinguish a real applicant from a
  manufactured one?
- When applications converge on the same AI-generated look, how do you tell genuine signal from
  sameness?
- How do you detect a manipulated or machine-generated application without false-flagging
  honest people?
- How do you surface candidates who deserve attention fast, inside an existing recruiting
  workflow rather than replacing it?

### Side B — Trust going out: impersonation and candidate scams

Bad actors register look-alike domains to send fake offer letters, run deepfake recruiter
calls, and scam job seekers out of money and data. Employers have almost no way to detect it or
warn candidates.

**Challenge:** make it harder to impersonate a real employer, and easier for a candidate to
verify that an offer or a recruiter is genuine.

Questions worth chasing:
- How would a candidate confirm that an offer or outreach is legitimately from the company?
- Can impersonating domains be detected and flagged early?
- What does a lightweight "trust signal" look like on a careers page or an offer letter?

### What a strong build looks like

A working tool that raises trust in the funnel and fits how hiring actually happens — a triage
or screening layer that plugs into an existing applicant tracking system rather than replacing
it, or a candidate/employer verification tool that makes impersonation harder to pull off and
easier to catch. Judges care more about a real, demonstrable solution to a narrow slice of the
problem than a broad concept that doesn't run.

No HR/recruiting-software experience required — engineers/builders go straight at detection,
identity, and verification problems; HR-tech practitioners (TA operators from the ~350-member
SAI community) will be in the room to keep builds grounded in how hiring really works.
